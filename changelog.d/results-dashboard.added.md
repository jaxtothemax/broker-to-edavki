- **Results dashboard in the browser app**: after your files and details, the app opens a dashboard with a sidebar (a bar along the bottom on a phone). Its overview leads with the tax to pay for the year, as an estimate, with the two returns it comes from beside it, and offers each return's download. A note that stops one return withholds that return only, and never hides the dashboard. Separate pages list your gains, dividends and notes. This replaces the review and download steps. The guided tour walks through the dashboard, and the Slovenian wording of its new labels is a draft.
