- **Guided tour: its lines no longer cross text**: the line from an explanation to what it explains used to run straight through whatever lay between, a file name or a date in the card or a figure behind it. Lines now go through the gaps, reach the target from whichever side is free, and keep clear of every word and control. Where no clear route exists the explanation is shown one at a time at the bottom, with a ring on its target, instead of with a line through the page. An explanation also no longer sits under the header at the top of the window: where its box would, the stop is shown one at a time instead. No computed figure changes.
