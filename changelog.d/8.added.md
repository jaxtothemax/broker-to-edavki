- **eToro account statement (XLSX), not yet checked against a real export with trades**: TaxReporter can read eToro's account statement, in the app and on the command line. US-dollar shares and ETFs held long go on Doh-KDVP, with every eToro position, whether closed or still open, matched first in, first out with your other brokers' lots of the same ISIN. Dividends go on Doh-Div, with the tax withheld. CFDs and other derivatives are left out with a note, crypto is set aside, and a CFD's dividend adjustments are counted with interest, which belongs on Doh-Obr. A statement is read only if it covers the account from its opening, and a second statement of the same account is refused. Refused with a finding that says why: shares bought short or with leverage, a position still open that paid an overnight fee, an activity type TaxReporter does not know, a dividend whose type or ISIN disagrees with its position, positions closed in part, shares or dividends in a currency other than US dollars, a trade so close to midnight that its day is unclear, an older layout, and a statement that does not start at the account's opening. A position still open whose ISIN the statement does not give is refused too, but it holds back only the Doh-KDVP of a year with a sale of any security from 30 days before its purchase on, never the Doh-Div, and says so in the years it changes nothing. A position still open whose ticker the statement gives two ISINs holds back both returns. The Glossary sheet that current statements carry is skipped, and a statement with no rows at all is read with a warning to export it again from the account's opening, in place of the empty one, if the account ever held anything.
