- **eToro account statement (XLSX), not yet checked against a real export**: TaxReporter can read eToro's account statement, in the app and on the command line. US-dollar shares and ETFs held long go on Doh-KDVP, with every eToro position, whether closed or still open, matched first in, first out with your other brokers' lots of the same ISIN. Dividends go on Doh-Div, with the tax withheld. CFDs, other derivatives and crypto are left out, each with a note. A statement is read only if it covers the account from its opening, and a second statement of the same account is refused. Refused with a finding that says why: shares bought short or with leverage, positions closed in part, shares or dividends in a currency other than US dollars, a trade so close to midnight that its day is unclear, and an older or edited statement.
