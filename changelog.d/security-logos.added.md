- **Company logos, and the company named on every dividend**: more securities now show their logo in the gains list and the dividends table, and a fund shows its fund house's wordmark whatever the fund: every Vanguard, iShares, VanEck or Amundi fund is recognized by the start of its name, not by an ISIN. A security with no logo of its own keeps its ticker tile. The logos are bundled with the app; none is fetched, so nothing about what you hold leaves your device. The dividends table now has a Security column (logo, ticker and name) beside the Payer column, so it stays clear which company's dividend it was when the payer on the form is your broker; the broker is no longer written twice when it is the payer. No figure on a return changes.
