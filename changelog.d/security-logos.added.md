- **Company logos, and the company named on every dividend**: more securities now show their logo in the gains list and the dividends table, and a fund shows its fund house's wordmark: a Vanguard, iShares, VanEck or Amundi fund is recognized by the start of its name, not by an ISIN. The wordmark appears only for a security the importer recognizes as a fund, so a company whose name starts with the same word keeps its ticker tile, as does any security with no logo of its own. The logos are bundled with the app; none is fetched, so nothing about what you hold leaves your device. The dividends table now has a Security column (logo, ticker and name) beside the Payer column, so it stays clear which company's dividend it was when the payer on the form is your broker; when the payer's name is the broker's own name, the broker is no longer written a second time beneath it. No figure on a return changes.
