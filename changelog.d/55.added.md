- **Trade Republic, more of its exports**: TaxReporter now reads the semicolon-separated form of Trade Republic's transaction export, which has a `value_date` column and no times, as well as the comma-separated form with or without quotes. Rows the reader did not know are now sorted explicitly. More transfer types, cash gifts and Saveback paid as cash are set aside with a reason. German tax rows (a tax refund, the Vorabpauschale) are set aside with one warning, as no Slovenian figure depends on them. Derivative trades are left out with a warning, as for Interactive Brokers. A child's (Junior) account is refused once, by name. Returns of capital, fund distributions, bond coupons and gold are refused for what they are. A row that is refused is now refused for its type even when its date and time disagree, instead of as an unreadable time. A savings plan in both forms of export, which name it differently, is read once. Trade Republic dividends are still not read.
