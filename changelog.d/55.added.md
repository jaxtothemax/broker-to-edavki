- **Trade Republic, more of its exports**: TaxReporter now reads the semicolon-separated form of Trade Republic's transaction export, which has a `value_date` column and no times, as well as the comma-separated form with or without quotes. Rows the reader did not know are now sorted explicitly. More transfer types, cash gifts and Saveback paid as cash are set aside with a reason. German tax rows (a tax refund, the Vorabpauschale) are set aside with one warning, which also counts sales Trade Republic taxed; no German tax is entered as tax paid abroad. Derivative trades are left out with a warning, as for Interactive Brokers. A child's (Junior) account is refused once, by name. A purchase whose amount includes a transaction tax is no longer refused. Returns of capital, fund distributions, bond coupons and gold are refused for what they are. A row that is refused is now refused for its type even when its date and time disagree, instead of as an unreadable time. A savings plan in both forms of export, which name it differently, is read once, as long as Trade Republic keeps its transaction ID from one export to the next; if it does not, the overlap stops the return instead of counting twice. Trade Republic dividends are still not read.
