- **Excel workbooks (XLSX), read safely**: TaxReporter can now open the XLSX files that eToro, XTB and Saxo export, with TaxReporter's own strict reader and no outside library. It reads only what a broker's workbook holds: the list of sheets, and the cells of a sheet when one is needed, each number read from its exact text to the 15 significant digits a spreadsheet keeps, and each date in the workbook's own date system. A file that is not laid out as Excel lays one out, that carries macros, or that would unpack to more than 64 MiB is refused with its reason; once a broker's sheets are read, a formula or a malformed cell is refused with the sheet and cell where reading stopped. eToro's account statement is the first workbook it recognizes (see its own entry), in the command line and in the web app, which now takes XLSX files.
