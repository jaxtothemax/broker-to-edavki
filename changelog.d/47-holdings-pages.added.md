- **Web app: what you still hold, and a page per broker account**: the results gain a **Holdings** page listing the shares your files still hold across all your brokers, with the purchases a next sale would use, oldest first: each with its cost in euros at the Banka Slovenije rate of its purchase date, and the day it moves to a lower holding-period rate (the day after its anniversary, safe however FURS counts that day). Holdings are as of the last day your files cover, never today, and show cost only, with no market prices. With two accounts or more, each has its own page, listed in the sidebar on a computer and at the top of Holdings on a tablet or phone, showing that account's own count of each security to check against your broker; an account whose files move shares is flagged "may not match your broker", since transfers are not read yet. The guided tour gains two stops for them. No computed figure changes: the returns and the estimate are the same.
