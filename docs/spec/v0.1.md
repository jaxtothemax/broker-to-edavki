# TaxReporter — product spec through v0.1

This spec is the input for `/import-spec docs/spec/v0.1.md` once the GitHub repository
exists. Each feature below becomes one issue. Phases become milestones. The research behind
every acceptance criterion is in `docs/research/`; the architecture is in `docs/adr/`.

**Goal of v0.1:** a Slovenian tax resident with **Trading 212**, **Interactive Brokers**
and/or **Trade Republic** accounts can produce **Doh-KDVP** and **Doh-Div** XML for **tax year 2026** that
import into eDavki without errors. Every amount is converted at the Banka Slovenije rate,
lots are matched FIFO per ISIN across every broker, and nothing leaves the user's computer.
Returns for tax year 2026 are due on **1 March 2027** (28 February 2027 is a Sunday, so ZDavP-2
Art. 45(2) moves the deadline to the next working day), so v0.1 must be usable by mid-January 2027.

**Out of scope for v0.1:** derivatives and CFDs (D-IFI), interest (Doh-Obr), brokers other
than Trading 212, IBKR and Trade Republic (added 2026-10-09, ADR 0015; its dividends wait for a
real export), filing on someone's behalf, and the LLM check.

---

## Phase: v0.1 — First filing season (tax year 2026)

### Banka Slovenije exchange-rate snapshot and lookup

**Problem:** FURS requires the rate published by Banka Slovenije for each transaction's own
date. BSI has no CORS, and its rates differ from ECB's on a few days.
**Users:** everyone, Maja and Luka first.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- A deterministic builder turns BSI `dtecbs-l.xml` and `EksotTecBS-l.xml` into a committed
  snapshot. It keeps the original decimal strings and records source URL, retrieval time,
  `Last-Modified` and SHA-256.
- Lookup returns rate and provenance using the last list on or before the date. Look-back
  is at most 10 days, after which it raises an explicit error. Currencies missing from the
  daily list fall back to the monthly list. Minor units are normalized: GBX/GBp → GBP/100.
- Known-answer tests pass:
  - NOK on 2025-10-23 = 11.8529
  - 2025-10-25 (Saturday) → the 2025-10-24 list
  - 2025-12-25 → the 2025-12-24 list
  - TWD on 2025-10-15 = 35.777 from the monthly list
  - BGN from 2026 → the fixed rate 1.95583
- A scheduled GitHub Action refreshes the snapshot daily, cross-checks it against ECB with an
  allow-list of the six known discrepancies, and opens a PR on change.
- `ATTRIBUTION.md` credits Banka Slovenije (CC BY 4.0) and states that values are unchanged.

### Normalized ledger, decimal money and diagnostics

**Problem:** each broker needs to be converted once into a common model, so that FX, FIFO
and XML are written and tested once.
**Users:** Tadej (contributors), and everyone indirectly.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- `packages/core` defines ledger events and their provenance: trade, dividend, withholding
  tax and its reversal, fee, corporate action (split/reverse split), transfer and ignored
  row.
- One decimal type is used for all money, quantities and rates. A lint rule or test forbids
  `number` arithmetic on them. Rounding happens only through per-field formatters.
- Diagnostics have a severity (info, warning, blocking), a stable code, a message and the
  source row. Blocking diagnostics prevent export.
- Overlapping imports of the same events are deduplicated by a stable event key.

### FIFO lot engine across brokers

**Problem:** FURS applies FIFO per security across all of a taxpayer's holdings, and no
existing tool does this across brokers.
**Users:** Luka, Mojca.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- Lots are matched FIFO per ISIN over merged events from every imported file and account.
- Splits and reverse splits adjust open lots without changing their acquisition dates.
- Each sale's lots record holding period and acquisition date. Lots held 15 years or more
  are excluded from the inventory lists.
- An estimate of the tax per holding-period bucket is computed and clearly labeled as an
  estimate. It uses the verified ZDoh-2 schedule: 25% under 5 years, 20% after 5, 15% after
  10, exempt after 15. Losses offset gains only within the year (no carry-forward, except the
  bonus-share case), and the net positive base is allocated pro rata to buckets
  (`docs/research/04-si-tax-rules.md`).
- A sale with insufficient purchase history raises a blocking diagnostic that asks for
  earlier exports, never a guess.

### Trading 212 CSV adapter

**Problem:** Trading 212 is the most common broker among Slovenian beginners. Its CSV has a
row per fractional purchase, and its columns vary between exports.
**Users:** Maja, Luka, Nina.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- Detection works by header. Columns are mapped by name, not position. There is a fixture
  for every known header revision listed in `docs/research/06-brokers-ibkr-t212-revolut.md`.
- The adapter handles:
  - market, limit and stop buys and sells;
  - `Stock split open`/`close` pairs;
  - dividend rows with withholding tax;
  - currency conversion fees;
  - fractional quantities up to 10 decimals.
- Deposits, withdrawals, interest and currency conversions become explicit ignored-or-later
  records. CFD accounts are refused with a diagnostic.
- Every input row is accounted for, and a test asserts the counts.

### Trade Republic CSV adapter

**Problem:** Trade Republic is the second broker among Slovenian investors (research 07 §2),
and since April 2026 offers a CSV transaction export; many of its users hold monthly savings
plans of funds. Its columns are known only from parser fixtures, at medium confidence.
**Users:** Maja, Luka.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- Detection by the exact 23-column header of `docs/research/07-brokers-eu-and-others.md`
  §4.2, in any order; a fixture for that revision (ADR 0015).
- Purchases, sales and savings-plan executions of shares and funds, dated by the UTC
  instant, which must fall on the export's own Berlin date; cash rows ignored by reason;
  dividends, free shares, corporate actions, foreign-leg trades and other assets refused
  with a finding each; every row accounted for, and a test asserts it.
- Before release: two overlapping real, anonymized exports pass and are read once, and they
  settle the open questions of ADR 0015 — whether a trade keeps its `transaction_id` across
  exports, whether `datetime` is the execution or the booking time, and what a dividend row's
  `amount`, `tax` and `original_amount` hold, so that dividends can be read.

### Interactive Brokers Flex Query adapter

**Problem:** IBKR is the main broker for active investors. Its Flex Query XML is rich but has
many event types.
**Users:** Luka.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- Documentation tells users exactly which Flex Query sections and fields to enable. The
  adapter validates that they are present.
- The adapter reads:
  - stock and ETF trades, with commissions in their own currency;
  - dividends and withholding tax, including reversals and corrections, linked to the
    dividend they adjust (payments in lieu are refused until their treatment is settled);
  - splits from corporate actions;
  - ISIN, currency and the listing exchange from securities info.
- Options, futures, CFDs and FX conversions are excluded with explicit records (D-IFI is a
  later milestone).
- XML parsing rejects DTDs and external entities.

### Doh-KDVP XML export

**Problem:** the inventory lists ("popisni listi") must follow `Doh_KDVP_9.xsd` exactly, plus
rules the XSD does not encode.
**Users:** everyone.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- One `KDVPItem` per security sold in the tax year, with a PLVP list of its relevant
  purchases and sales. Short-sale lists, if supported, use PLVPSHORT `F2` semantics.
- Field mapping follows the research:
  - F1–F11 as documented;
  - EUR per unit with 8 decimals;
  - running balance `F8` that never goes negative on a long list;
  - DocumentWorkflowID `O`.
- Business rules are enforced before export:
  - an 8-digit tax number;
  - at least one purchase and one sale per list;
  - empty typed elements are omitted.
- Golden files for at least five scenarios validate against the vendored XSD in CI: single
  lot, multiple lots, cross-broker, split, and a position partly sold across years following
  FURS FAQ 17.
- The two open conflicts listed in `docs/research/README.md` are settled with a primary
  source before the field mapping merges: what `true`/`false` in `F10` means, and whether
  commissions and transaction taxes enter the per-unit values.
- A handling of the 30-day loss rule (`F10`) is documented, with the source. The value is
  written only when it has been determined.

### Doh-Div XML export

**Problem:** each dividend must be reported with the foreign tax actually withheld, the
payer, the source country and the right type code.
**Users:** Maja, Luka, Nina.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- One row per payment, carrying:
  - gross EUR `Value` rounded to 2 decimals at the BSI rate of the payment date;
  - `ForeignTax` at the same rate, with `ForeignTax <= Value`;
  - payer name, address and country;
  - source country and an explicit type code.
- Same-day payments from one payer stay distinct, using different payer IDs.
- The relief statement for treaty credit uses the format documented in research. Credits
  above the treaty rate produce a warning that explains the excess must be reclaimed abroad.
- Golden files validate against `Doh_Div_3.xsd`.

### Web app: import, results, download

**Problem:** most filers are not technical and should not need to install anything or
upload their data.
**Users:** Maja, Nina, Mojca.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- A static app served under `/app/` on GitHub Pages lets the user drop T212, IBKR and Trade
  Republic files,
  enter their tax number and name, check the results and download both XML files.
- The results are a dashboard (#47, ADR 0018): a sidebar leading to an overview (the tax to pay
  as an estimate, and each return's download, withheld on its own while a note stops it) and
  to pages for the gains, the dividends and the notes; holdings and a section per broker
  account follow.
- Parsing runs in a Web Worker. No network request carries user data, which a test checks
  by asserting the app makes no outbound requests during an import.
- The results show, per security, each purchase and sale with its EUR value, the rate, and
  the rate's list date. Dividends are shown with foreign tax. Diagnostics are grouped by
  severity.
- The UI is available in Slovenian and English and is keyboard accessible, meeting WCAG 2.1
  AA.

### Command-line interface

**Problem:** power users and accountants want scriptable, reproducible runs.
**Users:** Luka, Mojca.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- `taxreporter import <files...> --year 2026 --tax-number … --out <dir>` produces the same XML
  as the web app for the same input, payer details included (the web app presets Trading 212's
  payer, the command line does not yet: #43). A shared golden test enforces this.
- The exit code is non-zero when blocking diagnostics exist. `--json` prints diagnostics in
  machine-readable form.
- `--json` also carries the holdings (ADR 0018): each account's positions, and the open FIFO
  lots across accounts with their EUR cost and next holding-period date.

### Audit report

**Problem:** filers and accountants need to explain every figure if FURS asks.
**Users:** Mojca, Luka, Nina.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- A CSV report and a printable HTML report list every event with its source row, rate and
  provenance, matched lots, and diagnostics.
- CSV cells are protected against formula injection.

### FURS schema drift check

**Problem:** FURS edits its XSDs in place without changing the version number.
**Users:** maintainers, and everyone indirectly.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- A scheduled workflow downloads the live XSDs for KDVP, Div and EDP-Common, compares their
  SHA-256 with the vendored copies, and opens an issue on any difference.

### User guide for the first filing season

**Problem:** users need step-by-step help that covers exporting from the broker, using the
app, and importing into eDavki.
**Users:** Maja.
**Acceptance criteria:**
- Guides cover how to export from Trading 212, from IBKR (the Flex Query setup) and from Trade
  Republic, how to
  import into eDavki (Dokumenti → Uvoz), how to review the result, and what to attach for
  foreign tax.
- A FAQ covers the deadline (1 March 2027 for tax year 2026), the mandatory e-filing
  thresholds (more than 10 transactions for Doh-KDVP, more than 5 payments for Doh-Div), the
  €250–400 fines, self-reporting, and what the tool does not handle.

## Phase: v0.2 — More brokers and the optional check

### eToro adapter
**Problem:** eToro is widely used in Slovenia, and its statement mixes real assets and CFDs.
**Acceptance criteria:** reads the XLSX account statement. Real stocks and ETFs go to KDVP/Div,
and CFDs are excluded with explicit records. Fixtures cover each known statement revision.

### Robinhood support
**Problem:** Robinhood serves EU customers through tokenized stocks, whose tax treatment in
Slovenia is unclear.
**Acceptance criteria:** a `docs/research/` note establishes from primary sources how these
products are taxed and which form they belong on, before any adapter is written.

### Revolut adapter
**Problem:** Revolut's trading CSV has changed format several times.
**Acceptance criteria:** there are fixtures per known revision, and amounts with currency
prefixes are parsed.

### Optional LLM second-opinion check
**Problem:** users want a sanity check without trusting a model with their figures.
**Acceptance criteria:** implemented as described in ADR 0008. It is off by default and uses
the user's own key. The consent screen shows the exact redacted payload, and findings are
advisory only.

### Doh-Obr interest export
**Problem:** interest paid by IBKR, Trading 212 or Trade Republic on cash goes on Doh-Obr, not the €1,000-exempt
form for deposit interest.
**Acceptance criteria:** Doh-Obr XML validates against `Doh_Obr_2.xsd`, with the type codes
from research.
